Hi, I'm Sidney.

I'm a vocalist, songwriter, actor and vocal coach based in Folkestone. As a recording and performing artist I've toured the UK and worked across television, radio, live music and the studio: fronting bands, releasing my own music and collaborating with composers and musicians. My screen work includes a vocal credit on Tiptoe, the Russell T Davies series.

I've trained with vocal coaches and musicians including Kate Cameron, Gareth Malone, Nicola Palmer and ILĀ, and I teach privately and at Seaview Studios.

Great vocal coaching isn't about making every singer sound the same. It's about understanding your instrument, discovering what makes your voice unique, and having the freedom to use it.

What I offer

Your voice is individual. Your coaching should be too. Every session is shaped around what you want to achieve, combining technique with creativity and real experience.

  1. 01

    Vocal technique & development

  2. 02

    Contemporary & alternative vocals

    The sounds that make your favourite artists distinctive: powerful belts, expressive rasp, breathy indie textures and mixed voice, built with coordination, control and vocal health.

  3. 03

    Jazz, musical theatre & choral

    Phrasing, interpretation, dynamics and stylistic awareness, whether it's your first jazz standard, a musical theatre audition or refining choral technique.

  4. 04

    Performance & confidence

    Stage presence, storytelling and microphone technique. Connect with your material, manage nerves and communicate with an audience.

  5. 05

    Songwriting & artist development

    Lyrics, melody and musical identity. Develop original material, find your artistic direction and build confidence in the music you make.

  6. 06

    Recording & studio technique

    Use professional recording equipment to develop mic technique and consistency, record your progress and learn to translate your voice to the studio.

What students say

Technique meets individuality.

Some students come to me to build range or prepare for a demanding performance. Others are finding their voice for the first time, rebuilding confidence, or just want a space to explore music without judgement. There's no single path, so I shape each session around your goals, the music you love and the way you learn, and keep the joy of making music at the centre of it.

In every session

  • A warm, supportive environment
  • Exercises tailored to your voice
  • Clear, practical feedback
  • Space for creativity and expression
  • Techniques to practise between lessons
  • Confidence, musicality and steady progress

Good questions

Do I need any experience?
No. Complete beginners are very welcome. We start from where you are, build healthy technique and confidence, and move at a pace that feels right.
What styles do you teach?
Indie, rock and alternative, pop and contemporary, jazz, musical theatre and choral. Lessons focus on what suits your voice and the music you love.
Who do you teach?
Singers from age 12 upwards: complete beginners, indie and rock vocalists, jazz singers, musical theatre and choral performers, songwriters, and people coming back to singing after a break.
Where are lessons held?
In my private studio in Folkestone, or online if you're further away or prefer to sing from home. I also teach at Seaview Studios.
Can you help me prepare for an audition, gig or recording?
Yes. We can build sessions around the date: song choice, storytelling, performance and keeping your voice consistent under pressure.
