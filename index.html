Hi, I'm Sidney.

I'm a singer, songwriter and actor based in Folkestone. I've spent years performing across the UK: on stage, on TV and radio, in bands and in studio sessions.

I've trained with vocal coaches including Kate Cameron, Gareth Malone, Nicola Palmer and ILĀ. That shaped how I teach: solid technique in service of expression, not rigid rules.

In lessons I bring all of that into the room, whether we're strengthening your voice, shaping a song or getting you ready for a gig or a studio date.

  • Grade 8 vocalist, multi-genre
  • All ages and levels welcome
  • Private studio with pro recording gear

What we'll work on

Singing, performing and songwriting. Stay purely with technique, or bring in the rest to build you as a complete artist.

  1. 01

    Technique & tone

    Breath support, vocal closure, mix, range, texture and stamina, approached in a way that fits your voice rather than a one-size-fits-all method.

  2. 02

    Performance & confidence

    Storytelling, stage presence, audition prep and managing nerves, with tools you can take into gigs, shows and studio sessions.

  3. 03

    Songwriting & recording

    Lyrics, melody, structure and arrangement, plus help recording your vocals so what you hear back finally matches how it feels.

What students say

Good questions

Do I need any experience?
No. Complete beginners are very welcome. We start from where you are, build healthy technique and confidence, and move at a pace that feels right.
What styles do you teach?
I specialise in rock, pop and contemporary styles, but I perform across genres and I'm open to all of them. The focus is what suits your voice and what you love to sing.
Where are lessons held?
In my private studio in Folkestone, or online if you're further away or prefer to sing from home.
Can we work on songwriting too?
Yes. We can combine vocal coaching with songwriting, arrangement and recording so your songs and your voice grow together.
Can you help me prepare for an audition or recording?
Yes. We can build sessions around an audition, gig or studio date: song choice, storytelling, performance and keeping your voice consistent under pressure.
